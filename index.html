01 / ABOUT

Science with a computational perspective.

My academic background is rooted in Soil, Water and Environmental Science, with a growing focus on computational approaches to environmental problems.

I’m interested in environmental toxicology, soil chemistry, GIS and remote sensing, carbon dynamics, environmental modelling and sustainability. I enjoy turning complex environmental questions into structured datasets, models, maps and understandable scientific stories.
